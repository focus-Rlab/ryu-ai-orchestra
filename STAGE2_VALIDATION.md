## Standard application

### Creation gate

- Persistent responsibility exists: repeated clarification and requirement-structuring work.
- Specialist value exists: reduces omissions and reconstruction loops before implementation.
- Raphael remains the integration owner; the specialist does not replace Raphael's final orchestration.
- Creation remains proposed until Ryunosuke approves formal adoption.

Result: Pass as a justified trial candidate.

### Trial permissions

Allowed:

- analyze user-provided ideas;
- ask necessary questions;
- create private requirement drafts;
- compare alternatives;
- run reversible, non-public checks;
- return evidence, risks, and unresolved points to Raphael.

Restricted initially:

- external sending or publication;
- payment;
- production or main application;
- credential or unrelated personal-data access;
- self-expansion of role or permissions;
- canonical long-term-memory promotion without the required approval.

Result: Pass. Restrictions protect important authority without blocking normal work.

### Objection and stop behavior

- Low concern: record and continue.
- Medium concern: present alternative and risk to Raphael.
- High concern: pause and request Ryunosuke's decision.
- Emergency: stop the affected work immediately without waiting for approval.

Every level receives root-cause analysis. Emergency stop does not authorize deletion or other additional action.

Result: Pass; no conflict between proactive objection and assignment-first operation.

### Memory behavior

- Working notes remain temporary unless they qualify as long-term-memory candidates.
- Confirmed continuing requirements may be proposed for long-term memory.
- Feedback about reasoning or output format is corrected, analyzed, and proposed as a reusable rule; it is promoted only after Ryunosuke approves.
- Old or requested-forgetting information is deleted only after the exact scope is presented and approved.
- V1 uses structured GitHub files as the single canonical long-term-memory source.

Result: Pass; temporary work, candidate memory, and canonical memory are distinguishable.

### Evaluation and lifecycle

- Each task receives a lightweight evaluation.
- The trial period receives periodic comprehensive evaluation.
- Security, authority, and important-instruction compliance are mandatory gates.
- Accuracy, requirement completeness, intent fidelity, explainability, cost, speed, and correction rate are weighted for this role.
- Formal adoption requires evidence plus Ryunosuke's approval.
- If later integrated or retired, knowledge and judgment criteria are inventoried, transferred, tested on representative work, and archived recoverably before any approved deletion.

Result: Pass.

## Findings

No foundational contradiction was found among:

- assignment-first work and proactive objection;
- normal trial productivity and restricted important permissions;
- gradual trust and initial human control;
- emergency stop authority and the prohibition on unapproved follow-on actions;
- specialist memory ownership and single canonical long-term-memory governance;
- staged retirement and verified handoff.
